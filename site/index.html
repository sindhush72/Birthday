Welcome, My Love

My Dearest,

In a garden of a thousand stars,

I found the one that never fades — you.

Every heartbeat hums your name,

every butterfly that dances by

carries a whisper of my love to you.

Through every dawn and every dusk,

my heart is yours, forever and a day.

Forever yours ♥
